About

I am a GIS analyst based in Kaduna, Nigeria, mapping conflict, climate and humanitarian risk across Nigeria, the Sahel and the Lake Chad Basin.

Field work with INSO Nigeria and Action Against Hunger taught me that a map has to be accurate, quick to read and useful to people working in difficult places. I have contributed spatial analysis to research published by CTC Sentinel at West Point and the Danish Institute for International Studies, and volunteered on marine Key Biodiversity Area data with BirdLife International.

I hold an MSc in GIS from Sheffield Hallam University and work mostly in QGIS, ArcGIS Pro and Python, with ACLED, HDX, GRID3, WPdx and CHIRPS data.
